USPS free branded boxes + envelopes

Brand-new, service-branded boxes, flat-rate and padded envelopes, paks and pouches - shipped to your door free, or picked up at any Post Office counter.

  • Order from the Postal Store's 'Free Shipping Supplies' listing: singles, or packs of 10 and 25, shipped to you at no cost.
  • 47 free products were listed in USPS's free-supplies filter as observed on 2026-11-02 - all Priority Mail or Priority Mail Express branded.
  • Key rule per USPS's own store text: 'Priority Mail Flat Rate shipping supplies may only be used to ship via Priority Mail Flat Rate' - service-branded supplies go with the matching service.
  • You pay postage as usual; the packaging itself is $0. Orders of 1-5 flat-rate items can be fulfilled by your local Post Office; bigger batches ship from distribution centers.

USPS Flat Rate strategy (+ observed prices)

Free flat-rate packaging with one fixed price regardless of weight (up to 70 lb) or distance - a winner for dense, heavy items shipping far.

  • Observed USPS-listed commercial prices on 2026-11-02: Flat Rate envelope $11.97; Legal envelope $12.51; Small Flat Rate box $12.95; Medium boxes $22.02 (retail $25.80); Large box $32.75 (retail $36.10); APO/FPO box $31.34.
  • Retail window pricing is higher - book postage online (Click-N-Ship or any discount platform) for commercial rates.
  • Flat Rate shines for heavy-to-far-zone lanes (book lots, tools, cast iron). It usually LOSES for light parcels vs. Ground Advantage tiers.
  • The Padded Flat Rate Envelope is the sleeper pick: clothing resellers can fill it generously and still pay the envelope price.

USPS supply rules (reuse + misuse)

90 seconds of fine print that keeps you out of trouble: branded supplies ship the matching service, old labels die, and misuse is federal-law territory per USPS.

  • DMM 115 wording (Priority Mail Express): USPS-provided packaging 'must be used only for Priority Mail Express' - the same style of rule applies to Priority Mail packaging under DMM 135.
  • USPS's own FAQ on using and reusing boxes adds: 'Misuse may be a violation of federal law.'
  • Reusing USPS-branded boxes: allowed only for the matching service, and old labels/barcodes must be fully removed or blacked out.
  • Never put Priority-branded packaging under FedEx, UPS, or Ground Advantage postage; never resell or give supplies away as materials.

USPS Ground Advantage - bring your own box

The workhorse for 1-16 oz reseller parcels: weight-tier pricing, any sturdy box or mailer allowed, and no free branded supplies - pair it with the store give-aways and frugal picks below.

  • Any plain, service-neutral box or poly/bubble mailer is acceptable; old labels must be removed or crossed out.

Grocery + big-box produce/drink cases

Banana boxes, egg flats and soda cases are free, sturdy, slotted, and they stack like bricks - the classic first fifty boxes for a new reseller.

  • Best picks: banana boxes (stable footprint, vented - line one with a fresh garbage bag), egg flats (built-in dividers), drink and produce cases.
  • Script that works: 'Hi! I ship packages - could I take some of those empty produce boxes off your hands?' Nearly everyone says yes.
  • Go after restock windows (early morning), offer to take a whole stack, and become a regular - stores like fewer bales to haul.
  • Wipe down and sniff test: bakery and produce boxes can carry odors into collectibles. Skip anything greasy, wet, or pest-scarred.

Level zero: reuse your own inbound packaging

Every Amazon box and padded mailer you receive is reseller gold - if you strike the labels and match the lane.

  • Remove or fully black out old labels and barcodes - scanners can resurrect them and snarl tracking.
  • Service-branded inbound gets reused only for the same service (a Priority box only ships Priority again).
  • Plain brown boxes are open games for Ground Advantage and every ground courier.
  • Padded mailers usually keep their bubble intact; peeling a new outer label keeps them presentable.
  • Skip moldy, wet, or odd-smelling boxes - your five-star condition reputation starts at the bench.

The honest fine print

  • Every price here was observed on 2026-11-02 and will drift. Re-check before big orders - postage pages are the source of truth.
  • Service-branded free packaging (USPS Priority, FedEx One Rate, UPS express) exists so you ship that service. Using it for another carrier is misuse - see the USPS supply-rules card.
  • No affiliate links anywhere in this project; links go to the program or product page itself. Store give-aways are local folk practice, marked “call ahead.”
